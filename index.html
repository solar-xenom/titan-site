Save a spreadsheet as CSV with these columns. Rows with a SKU that already exists get updated. New SKUs get added.

sku,name,category,price,qty,barcode,preorder,release_date,game PKM-SV-ETB,Pokémon Scarlet & Violet Elite Trainer Box,tcg,49.99,6,820650851234,,,pokemon DND-PHB24,Player's Handbook (2024),dnd,49.95,3,9780786969517,,,dnd PKM-NEXT-BB,Next Pokémon set booster box,tcg,149.99,0,,yes,2026-12-05,pokemon

Category must be one of: dnd, tcg, models, board, collectibles, accessories. The last four columns are optional. Put "yes" under preorder for coming-soon items. Under game, put the game's name or short code, like magic, pokemon, yugioh, or dnd.
